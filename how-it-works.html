How it works

A simple cycle that replaces scattered updates

Whether the job is writing a report or inspecting a site, the process stays the same.

Manager assigns

Create the task, set the owner, deadline, instructions, and any required proof.

Employee works

Opens My Tasks, reads the brief, updates status, and adds progress notes.

Proof submitted

Photos, documents, checklist results, and notes are attached and submitted for review.

Manager reviews

Approves the work or returns it with feedback. History is kept for both sides.

For managers

See open work, overdue items, and the review queue in one dashboard. Stop chasing people on WhatsApp for status updates.

For employees

Clear instructions, one place to submit proof, and straightforward feedback when something needs correction.
